>The Collapse.
>
>The earth opened up and swallowed the city of Corvald whole. The capital disappeared in an instant, along with the thousands of souls who lived there, leaving nothing but a hole in the blackened ground, kilometers wide and impossibly deep. Around its edge the town of Luden’s Rest took root as people flocked to investigate. Baffled scholars, brave adventurers, knights sworn to protect the peace, thieves looking to find and loot the ruins - these were the people that became the very first Divers, and they soon learned that going into the Void was all too often a one-way trip. 
>
>Now, ten years later, it’s your turn. The Void Divers’ Guild is launching its 27th Official Expedition and is seeking capable adventurers to join. Whether drawn by the strange and compelling pull of curiosity, motivated to end the flow of otherworldly monsters spat up from the earth, or in search of something or someone who could be down there, you have decided to risk your life and brave the unforgiving depths of the Void.
