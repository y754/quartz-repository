>[!note|no-t no-i center text-center]
>Stories of ghosts and hauntings are simply your reality living in the small town of Willowshore. Some may be true, some may be tall tales told to children to get them to behave. But every summer, these tales inch closer to your home as hauntings, spirits, and undead sightings increase. While the cause is uncertain, these ghosts pose a real danger, luring people to their deaths.
>
>To ward them off, an annual celebration called the Reenactment Festival is held on the last day of spring; four people are chosen to be snatched away by “ghosts” as a farce to trick the real ghosts that this settlement is already haunted, and thus convince them to seek a happier village to inflict misery upon.
>
>This year, the duty has fallen to you, and on the night of the festival, you and your companions are whisked off. But when you wake up on that first day of summer, you find that this time around, the festival did _not_ work, and your hometown has been plunged into the Season of Ghosts.

_Season of Ghosts_ is a Pathfinder 2e Adventure Path with folkloric horror and mystery themes! The bulk of the campaign is set in the small town of [Willowshore](https://lore.karma.computer/campaigns/season-of-ghosts/willowshore), located in the haunted nation of Shenmen.
