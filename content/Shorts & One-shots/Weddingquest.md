> _You are cordially invited to the winter wedding of Ophelia von Henneberg and Maxim St Clair, to be held at the House in the Valley, where you are welcome to stay the nights preceding and following the event._
>

Still, wizards never were the most conventional, and not many get a chance to look in on the House in the Valley — as long as the pair keep their work from their wedding, anyway.
