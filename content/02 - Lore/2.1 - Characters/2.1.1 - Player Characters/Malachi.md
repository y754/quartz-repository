## Description
Built like a wolfhound, all long limbs and deep chest, Malachi stands at around 7’4” tall, looming over most people. Her skin is pale olivine grey-green, marked with malachite tattoos, scars, and body paint in blues and greens. Her hair is the colour of slate, her eyes green, dark enough to be mistaken for black in all but the brightest daylight.

She’s a habitual sloucher, a lifetime of leaning down to talk and listen taking its toll on her posture. She wears a suit of finely-fitted leather armour, strips of hide tanned dark silver, forming a butter-soft second skin that she never removes. Getting close - if you can - reveals the armour carries a subtle patterning, similar to that of her own skin, greys layered on one another with faint marks like scars tracing the stitching.
