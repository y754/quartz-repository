## Description
You see a tall man (at least, you think he’s a man, it’s _complicated_), wearing a white fox mask with red paint around the eyes which covers the upper half of his face. From what you can see of his face, he has pallid, greyish skin, with a serene, almost-placid smile. When he speaks, you could almost see the tips of his half-orc tusks, typically hidden due to its small size. He has long, black, silky hair, with half of it pulled up in a top knot. He’s wrapped in a white pleated hanfu with bloodred embroidery and hemming. There’s a delicate grace to him when he moves, almost gliding, as if he’s incorporeal. But unfortunately for you _and_ for him, he is very much real and alive.
